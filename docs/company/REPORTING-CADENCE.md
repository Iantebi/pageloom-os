# Executive reporting cadence

Reports use persisted records, name their reporting window and currency, show comparisons where available, and never substitute pipeline for revenue. Each report records its generator, source cutoff, assumptions, exceptions, and owner review.

| Cadence | Decision purpose | Minimum KPIs |
|---|---|---|
| Daily | Protect delivery and cash today | cash alerts, new/qualified leads, active/blocked projects, overdue approvals, incidents, failed backups/deployments, support severity, external actions awaiting approval |
| Weekly | Manage flow and capacity | conversion by stage, pipeline value, delivery cycle time, stage aging, revisions, utilization, receivables, MRR movement, customer/project health, support load, infrastructure/AI cost |
| Monthly | Close and improve the business | revenue, expenses, contribution/net profit, cashflow variance, MRR/ARR, churn/retention, CLV, cost and margin per customer, forecast variance, uptime, incidents, backup/restore status |
| Quarterly | Allocate capital and change strategy | cohort retention, channel economics, capacity, pricing realization, service profitability, risk trend, security/DR exercises, hiring case, roadmap outcomes |
| Annual | Set operating plan | audited/approved financial summary, recurring revenue, cash runway, customer outcomes, market/channel performance, staffing, infrastructure forecast, major risks and annual objectives |

Every red metric has an accountable owner, cause, action, due date, and verification method. Absence of data is reported as unavailable—not zero or green.
