# CEO operating handbook

## Daily

Review cash alerts, critical incidents, failed backups/deployments, customer/project health, overdue approvals, high-priority support, today’s calls, and protected actions. Resolve ownership and customer impact before starting discretionary work. Never approve a release without its immutable QA evidence and rollback target.

## Weekly review

Review pipeline and conversion, delivery flow and stalled stages, receivables, margin by active project, support load, infrastructure cost, upcoming renewals/expiries, incidents, and the launch board. Choose at most three company priorities and assign one accountable owner to each.

## Monthly review

Close the books with the accountant process; examine profit, cash runway, MRR movement, retention, customer profitability, forecast variance, capacity, quality and availability. Review risk controls and customer feedback. Approve policy/rule changes through version control.

## Quarterly planning

Compare outcomes to strategy; evaluate pricing, segment fit, channels, capacity, architecture, security, disaster recovery and hiring. Fund only work tied to customer outcome, risk reduction, margin, or a tested growth constraint. Retire initiatives without evidence.

## Annual planning

Set the customer, revenue, margin, cash, retention, quality, security and team objectives. Build downside/base/upside forecasts. Confirm insurance, legal documents, vendors, recovery plans, data retention, and statutory obligations with qualified advisers.

## Decision framework

State the decision, deadline, reversible/irreversible nature, customer impact, evidence, alternatives, downside, cost, legal/security implications, and success measure. Consult the accountable expert. Prefer reversible experiments; require owner approval for production, billing, payment, legal, external messages, domains and destructive operations. Record the decision and revisit trigger.

## Risk, hiring, financial and growth reviews

- Risk: top exposures, control effectiveness, near misses, concentration, recovery readiness and overdue mitigation.
- Hiring: proven capacity constraint, six-month affordable base case, role outcome, scorecard, legal employment review, onboarding and manager capacity.
- Finance: reconciled actuals, tax reserve, cash runway, receivables, unit economics, forecast variance and commitments.
- Growth: qualified demand, conversion, acquisition cost, referral rate, retention, delivery capacity and margin. Never buy growth that operations cannot serve.
