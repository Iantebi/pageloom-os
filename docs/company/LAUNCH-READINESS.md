## Before Customer #1

1. **P0 / Legal / 1–3 days:** counsel and owner approve agreement, privacy, accessibility, terms/DPA and electronic-signature consent.
2. **P0 / CEO + accountant / 0.5–1 day:** approve launch offer, payment/tax workflow, refund position, invoice provider and tax-reserve treatment.
3. **P0 / CEO / 2 hours:** approve support channel, business hours, severity promises, maintenance scope and emergency contact.
4. **P0 / Operations / 0.5 day:** run the named-customer preflight using the first-customer runbook; confirm backup, release, rollback, access and data-processing records.
5. **Per release / CEO / 15 minutes:** approve production deployment only after QA evidence and rollback target.

## Roadmap after first customer

- **Epsilon—Controlled acceptance:** close external gates, execute Customer #1, measure time/margin/friction, fix only acceptance defects.
- **Next 3 customers:** validate price, scope, revisions, support load, restore drill and customer handoff; standardize observed exceptions.
- **10 customers:** event-maintained aggregates, independent security/accessibility review, accountant-validated automation, delegate/access continuity.
- **50+ customers:** fleet inventory collectors, SLO/error budgets, dedicated operations ownership and tested customer infrastructure portability.

## Weak points hardened in Delta

- Replaced fragmented business assumptions with a versioned executable policy contract.
- Added authoritative ownership/review rules and a functional knowledge map instead of duplicate runbooks.
- Made report data absence explicit and tied every red result to corrective ownership.
- Removed the report-loader hook dependency warning by stabilizing its authenticated query callback.

No production deployment, legal publication, billing change, external message, or destructive operation was performed.
