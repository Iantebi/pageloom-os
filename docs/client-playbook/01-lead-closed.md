# 01 — Lead Closed

## Goal

Confirm the deal is real — price, scope, and payment terms agreed — before any customer
record is created in PageLoom OS.

## Owner Tasks

- Agree scope and price with the customer (call, proposal, or however the agency sells).
- Confirm payment terms (deposit received, or invoicing agreed).
- Collect the four things you'll need for the next stage: the business's name, a contact person's name, their email, and their phone number.

## Customer Tasks

- Agree to the proposal and provide their contact details.
- Complete any agreed payment step (deposit, first invoice, etc.).

## Checklist

- [ ] Price and scope are agreed and, ideally, in writing.
- [ ] Payment terms are confirmed.
- [ ] You have the business name, contact name, email, and phone number ready.

## Exit Criteria

The deal is closed and you have the four fields needed for [02 Create Client](./02-create-client.md). This stage never touches PageLoom OS itself — however the agency closes deals today (a CRM lead, a call, a signed proposal) is unaffected by everything that follows.
