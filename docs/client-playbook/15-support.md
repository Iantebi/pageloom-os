# 15 — Support

## Goal

Keep the customer relationship healthy after launch: fast answers to support requests,
and a clear renewal path for ongoing maintenance.

## Owner Tasks

- Respond to support tickets within the agreed response time for their priority level.
- Track maintenance renewal dates and reach out before they lapse, not after.
- Watch for signs of customer inactivity or dissatisfaction and follow up proactively rather than waiting for a complaint.

## Customer Tasks

- Open a support ticket (from their portal) for anything from a small content tweak to a bug report — priority (critical/high/normal/low) sets the expected response time.
- Let PageLoom know ahead of a renewal if their needs are changing (more pages, new features, etc.).

## Checklist

- [ ] Every open support ticket has a response within its priority's target time.
- [ ] Maintenance renewal is tracked and the customer is reminded before it's due.
- [ ] Recurring requests are noticed and addressed at the root (e.g. a training moment or a small process fix), not just resolved one ticket at a time.

## Exit Criteria

There is no formal exit from Support — it continues for as long as the customer
relationship does. A new significant engagement (a redesign, a second site) starts a new
pass through this playbook from [01 Lead Closed](./01-lead-closed.md).
