# 04 — Discovery Completed

## Goal

The customer answers PageLoom's 9-section Business Discovery questionnaire, giving the
team everything needed to start building — no phone call required to extract the basics.

## Owner Tasks

- Do nothing unless asked — the flow is self-service. You'll get an in-app notification the moment the customer starts, saves a draft, uploads a file, and finally submits.
- If a customer stalls partway, a friendly nudge (WhatsApp/email) usually works better than PageLoom re-sending the same link.

## Customer Tasks

- Work through all 9 sections at their own pace (business identity, customer audience, services, differentiation, trust, branding, materials/uploads, technical presence, goals) — autosaved after every change, so there's no risk of losing progress.
- Complete every required field — the product will not let them submit otherwise, and will highlight exactly what's missing.
- Upload real business photos if they have them; if not, they're explicitly told PageLoom can use AI-generated or licensed stock images instead — never a blocker.
- Click through the final review screen and submit.

## Checklist

- [ ] All required fields across all 9 sections are complete.
- [ ] The submission actually succeeded (the customer sees the "thank you" completion screen, not an error).
- [ ] An in-app "Business Discovery submitted" notification appears in the Owner Workspace.

## Exit Criteria

The Discovery session's status is `submitted` (100% complete). Move to
[05 Build Preparation](./05-build-preparation.md).
