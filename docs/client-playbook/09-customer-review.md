# 09 — Customer Review

## Goal

Give the customer a clean, confident moment to review their finished website and decide:
approve, or ask for changes.

## Owner Tasks

- Confirm the preview link is live and working before the customer is notified.
- Do nothing else — the review card in the customer's portal handles the decision itself: "Your website is ready for review" with two clear actions, **Final Approval** and **Request Revisions**. Nothing about revision rounds is shown to the customer until they actually choose to request one.

## Customer Tasks

- Open the preview link and review every page carefully.
- Choose one:
  - **Final Approval** — proceed to [12 Final Approval](./12-final-approval.md).
  - **Request Revisions** — proceed to [10 Revision Round 1](./10-revision-round-1.md).

## Checklist

- [ ] The customer has been notified that the site is ready (link sent, or they can see it themselves in their portal).
- [ ] The live preview matches what passed Internal QA — nothing changed in between.

## Exit Criteria

The customer has made a decision. If they approved, skip to
[12 Final Approval](./12-final-approval.md). If they requested changes, continue to
[10 Revision Round 1](./10-revision-round-1.md).
