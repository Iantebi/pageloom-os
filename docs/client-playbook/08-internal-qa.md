# 08 — Internal QA

## Goal

Catch every mistake internally, before the customer ever sees the site — a customer's
first review should find nothing broken, only preferences to fine-tune.

## Owner Tasks

- Review every page on desktop and mobile — layout, spacing, images, and copy.
- Check every link, form, and call-to-action actually works.
- Verify the site reflects the customer's real business details (name, phone, email, address, hours) exactly as given in Discovery — typos here are the most common avoidable mistake.
- Confirm nothing placeholder-looking ("Lorem ipsum", stock phone numbers, sample testimonials) made it into the build.
- Only move to Customer Review once the site would pass as "ready" from the customer's own point of view.

## Customer Tasks

- None — this stage is entirely internal.

## Checklist

- [ ] Every page checked on both desktop and mobile.
- [ ] Every link, form, and button works.
- [ ] Business details match Discovery exactly.
- [ ] No placeholder content remains anywhere.

## Exit Criteria

The site is genuinely ready for the customer to see. Move to
[09 Customer Review](./09-customer-review.md).
