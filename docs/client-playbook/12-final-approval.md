# 12 — Final Approval

## Goal

Get an explicit, unambiguous "yes, publish this" from the customer before anything goes
live — never assume approval from silence or an informal "looks good" message.

## Owner Tasks

- Nothing — this is a self-service customer action. Once submitted, move to
  [13 Domain & Publish](./13-domain-and-publish.md).

## Customer Tasks

Before the site can be published, the customer must check all three of:

- [ ] "I have reviewed the website."
- [ ] "I approve the website."
- [ ] "I authorize PageLoom to connect the domain and publish the website."

Only once all three are checked does the confirm action become available — there is no
way to publish with only some of them checked.

## Checklist

- [ ] All three checkboxes were genuinely checked by the customer (not pre-filled or defaulted).
- [ ] The approval was recorded (the project shows a recorded approval timestamp).

## Exit Criteria

The customer's final approval is recorded. Move to
[13 Domain & Publish](./13-domain-and-publish.md).
