# 14 — Website Live

## Goal

Hand the finished website over cleanly: the customer knows what's live, who to contact,
and what happens next.

## Owner Tasks

- Record the handover: live URL, support instructions, maintenance information, and a clear split of PageLoom's ongoing responsibilities versus the customer's own.
- Confirm the customer can actually reach the live site and that it matches what they approved.
- Make sure the customer knows how to reach support (see [15 Support](./15-support.md)) before considering this stage closed.

## Customer Tasks

- Confirm they can see their site live at the real domain.
- Save the support contact/instructions provided.

## Checklist

- [ ] Handover record is complete (live URL, support instructions, maintenance info, responsibility split).
- [ ] The customer has confirmed they can see the live site.
- [ ] The customer knows how to request support or future changes.

## Exit Criteria

The customer has a live site and knows how to get help. Move to
[15 Support](./15-support.md) for anything that comes up afterward.
