# 07 — SEO

## Goal

Make sure the site is actually findable — titles, meta descriptions, headings, and
structure aligned with how the customer's real customers search.

## Owner Tasks

- Apply on-page SEO basics to every page: title tags, meta descriptions, heading structure, image alt text.
- Use the customer's own service area and service names from Discovery (`presence` and `services` sections) rather than generic keywords.
- Confirm technical basics: sitemap, robots rules, and page load performance are acceptable before moving on.

## Customer Tasks

- None, unless the Owner needs to confirm a specific local service area or business name variant used in search.

## Checklist

- [ ] Every page has a unique, relevant title and meta description.
- [ ] Heading structure is logical (one H1 per page, meaningful H2s).
- [ ] Service area and service names match what the customer actually said in Discovery.
- [ ] No broken links or missing alt text on images.

## Exit Criteria

The site is optimized and ready for PageLoom's own internal review. Move to
[08 Internal QA](./08-internal-qa.md).
