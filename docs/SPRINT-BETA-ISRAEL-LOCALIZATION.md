# Sprint Beta — Israel Localization

## Completed

- Hebrew-first customer and project locale defaults
- Full client-session RTL and Hebrew portal navigation
- Hebrew authentication, portal, upload, review, revision, approval, and legal-center experiences
- Hebrew email and WhatsApp draft templates with owner-controlled sending unchanged
- Versioned Hebrew proposal, contract, legal, invoice, and project-summary artifact contract
- Israeli tax-profile and invoice-draft foundation using ILS and integer agorot
- Automated localization, legal-version, and finance-calculation coverage

## External blockers

- Final Hebrew contracts, privacy policy, terms, accessibility statement, and consent text require approval by qualified Israeli legal counsel and the owner before publication.
- Tax invoice issuance requires selection and authorization of a compliant Israeli invoice provider and review by the company accountant. The platform currently prepares drafts only.
- WhatsApp template registration with Meta and any external email/WhatsApp send remain owner-controlled external actions.

No production deployment was performed as part of this sprint.
